# Business Resilience Model

## Purpose

Business Resilience measures whether the organization can continue operating safely when AI fails, behaves unexpectedly, is compromised or becomes unavailable.

## Target scale

0–100

## Candidate factors

- documented AI service dependencies
- provider/model concentration risk
- fallback or manual operating procedures
- ability to disable AI safely
- agent containment capability
- recovery and restoration authority
- logging and evidence preservation
- model/service outage preparedness
- resilience testing
- model degradation/drift handling
- business continuity integration
- incident communications

## Key question

> Can the business continue safely if a critical AI capability becomes unavailable or untrusted?

## Design principle

Resilience should measure both cyber-recovery capability and operational dependency. An organization may have strong incident response but poor resilience if core business processes cannot operate without one AI provider or model.
