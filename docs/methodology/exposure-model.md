# AI Exposure Model

## Purpose

AI Exposure measures how much business risk the organization carries because of the scope, criticality and access of its AI usage. Exposure is intentionally separate from control maturity.

## Target scale

0–100

Suggested interpretation:

- 0–20 — Limited
- 21–40 — Moderate
- 41–60 — Significant
- 61–80 — High
- 81–100 — Very High

## Candidate factors

- breadth of AI adoption
- sensitive-data access
- customer-facing exposure
- regulated or high-impact decisions
- business criticality
- privileged access
- systems reachable by AI
- use of autonomous agents
- third-party AI dependency
- provider concentration
- external/public accessibility

## Methodology principle

The score should measure exposure, not imply that AI adoption itself is negative. High exposure with high maturity may represent a well-controlled strategic investment; high exposure with low maturity should generate urgent priorities.

## Validation requirement

Weights should remain configurable until scenario testing and practitioner review are complete.
