# Assessment Domains

The full assessment is organized across ten domains.

## 1. AI Discovery & Inventory

Determine whether the organization understands its AI estate, including applications, models, agents, embedded SaaS AI, data sources, identities, tools and third-party dependencies.

## 2. Risk Classification & Business Impact

Determine whether AI systems are classified according to business criticality, data sensitivity, external exposure, privilege, autonomy, regulatory impact and human oversight.

## 3. Governance, Accountability & Decision Rights

Determine whether policies, accountable owners, human sponsors, approval authorities, residual-risk acceptance and AI lifecycle decision rights are explicit and operational.

## 4. AI Threat Modeling

Determine whether AI-specific attack paths and failure modes are incorporated into architecture and application-security reviews, including prompt injection, RAG manipulation, agent/tool abuse, model compromise and supply-chain risk.

## 5. Secure AI Development Lifecycle

Evaluate security across Design → Build → Test → Deploy → Operate → Retire, including threat modeling, provenance, secrets, dependencies, adversarial testing, deployment gates, monitoring and secure decommissioning.

## 6. Protection Controls

Evaluate technical controls across:

- Identity Security
- Data Security
- Model Security
- Application Security
- Agent Security
- Infrastructure & Supply Chain

## 7. Validation & Red Teaming

Determine whether critical AI systems undergo risk-based adversarial validation, prompt-injection testing, data-leakage testing, agent-abuse testing, privilege testing and resilience exercises.

## 8. Monitoring & Detection

Determine whether the organization can observe security-relevant AI activity, distinguish human from autonomous actions, detect abnormal behavior and reconstruct agent activity during investigations.

## 9. Incident Response & Business Resilience

Determine whether AI-specific incidents can be detected, contained, investigated and recovered from, and whether the business can operate safely through AI outages, degradation or loss of trust.

## 10. Measurement & Continuous Improvement

Determine whether AI security is measured through KPIs/KRIs, reassessed over time, reported to leadership and continuously improved as AI adoption, models, agents and dependencies change.

## Cross-cutting themes

The following should appear throughout multiple domains rather than being isolated into a single checklist:

- Third-party AI risk
- Agent identity and autonomy
- Accountability and decision rights
- Privacy
- Safety
- Reliability
- Regulatory/compliance risk
- Business resilience
